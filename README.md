## Current work

**[Cut Force: The Combine Trait the Stopwatch Misses](https://github.com/jekelle/cut-force-big-data-bowl-2027)**
NFL Big Data Bowl 2027, Open Track. A tracking-based measure of how hard a receiver turns at speed. It is a stable trait (reliability 0.59), independent of every timed drill, carries over to NFL routes (r = 0.23), and connects to targets. Public Kaggle notebook with 13 interactive charts and a Cut Force card for all 107 receivers. [Notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/jaykelley/cut-force-the-combine-trait-the-stopwatch-misses)

**Win probability, NFL to high school** (TESM-S 501, private)
Upgrading a logistic win probability model with a score-by-time spline interaction (18% lower Brier in the last five minutes of one-score games), then testing whether the game-state logic survives on real high school play-by-play. The NFL curve transfers. The team's level does not.

**Westfield Shamrocks analytics dashboard** (private)
A React app for in-game charting, film tags and weekly reports for a varsity football staff. Hudl exports in, one-page coach reports out.
